# ABOUT METALYX

METALYX is **not** a fabrication company.

METALYX is a **managed fabrication marketplace**.

Customers contact METALYX instead of searching for individual fabricators.

METALYX understands the customer's requirements, finds the best verified fabrication partner, manages quotations, coordinates the project, oversees communication, and ensures successful project completion.

Our fabrication partners focus on manufacturing.

METALYX focuses on customer experience, project management, quality, and trust.

Our vision is to become India's most trusted fabrication management platform.
